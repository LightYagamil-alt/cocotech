# Coco Tech Shine

Create a modern, professional, and visually appealing website for Coco Tech, a small-to-mid scale creative services business. Coco Tech specializes in:

Website creation for small and medium businesses

Social media post design and graphics

Video editing services

Website Goals:

Look legit, trustworthy, and cool

Showcase services clearly with examples or sample visuals

Include easy-to-find contact info

Highlight professionalism and creativity

Mobile-friendly and responsive design

Sections to include:

Home – Brief intro about Coco Tech, catchy tagline like “Creativity meets Technology”

About – Small paragraph about Coco Tech’s mission and vision

Services – Detail website creation, post creation, video editing, with icons or images

Portfolio / Samples – Visual examples of work (placeholder images if needed)

Contact – Phone: +256 785852378, email (if available), contact form

Optional Features:

Testimonials or client reviews

Call-to-action buttons like “Get a Quote” or “Hire Us Now”

Links to social media profiles

Make the design modern, clean, and appealing, with professional color schemes and smooth navigation. Add subtle animations or effects for a creative touch.
